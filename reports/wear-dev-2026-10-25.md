# Job W (C9) dev result: 23 seeds, 21 days, wear on vibration

Pre-registration: C9 and its radar-pedestal amendment in `docs/success-criteria.md` (2026-10-25). Run `w-dev-2026-10-25b`,
dev seeds 1-3 and 41-60, each with a healthy twin, offline on the day host. `dev`, not a verdict.

## Read against the rule

- **Arm (b), frozen reference: "not shown".** Three of four parts pass (median delay 4.1 d <= 5, hold 0.945 >= 0.90, vibration
  false alerts 0 per day <= 0.05), detection fails: 18 of 23 = 0.78 < 0.90.
- **Why the five misses (43, 47, 51, 53, 60): the reference was not healthy.** On every one, vibration sensor step or drift faults
  fall inside the 2-day reference window, so mu_R or sigma_R is wrong: mu_R 2.5-6.1 against a healthy level of 1.1-1.6, or sigma_R
  0.8-3.4 against the floor of 0.05. Then either the gap never reaches 4 (wide sigma), or wear run and twin both sit in alert from
  the start, so the wear cannot be told apart (wrong mu). The same thing cuts the hold on seed 1 (0.41). The rule is right about
  wear and wrong about where its reference comes from: a blind "first 2 days" reference is a single point of failure.
- **(b') slow re-baseline = (b).** Same detections, one hour later on one seed. The 30-day constant barely moves in 21 days.
- **Arm (a), online only: does not "adapt it away" by the letter of the rule** (7 of 23 runs flagged, >= 10 %). In substance it
  barely sees it: 11 wear-only unexplained alerts in all 23 runs (1-3 per flagged run, first one 1-16 days after onset), against 3
  the other way round (twin-only) by chance, among 500-1300 alerts per run. And wear makes the detector *quieter*: the wear run raises
  a median 35 % fewer vibration alerts than its twin, because the detector's scale grows with the noise and hides the bursts.
  Meanwhile its own one-step error rose to 1.79x the twin's (median, last ramp day): the error saw the wear, the alerts did not.
- **False alerts in healthy stretches:** vibration 0, temp 0, wind 0, pressure 0.047 per day, solar 0.074 per day. Caveat:
  vibration has many long sensor faults, so only 57 of its 437 twin-days count as healthy (87 alerts fell on long faults).

## H14

- "The online-only arm never flags it": **wrong by the letter** (7 of 23 runs had 1-3 wear-only alerts, above the chance rate),
  **right in substance** (no operator would read 1-3 unlabelled alerts among about a thousand as wear, and alerts fell by a third).
- "The reference comparison flags it with a delay of days": **right when the reference is clean** (median 4.1 d, range 2.3-11.4),
  wrong in 5 of 23 runs where faults poisoned the reference.
- Added guesses: online error ratio about F, **roughly right but lower** (1.79 vs median F 2.35); (b') later or misses, **wrong**
  (identical); pressure above 0.05 per day, **wrong** (0.047, just under), and solar, which was not predicted, is the one above it.
